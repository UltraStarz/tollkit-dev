How paying works

  • 1. AskYour agent calls a tool. Without a payment it gets the price back, not an error.
  • 2. PayIt signs a USDC payment on Base or Solana from its own wallet. No ETH or SOL needed for fees.
  • 3. Get the resultThe call runs and settles. If it fails, nothing is charged.
