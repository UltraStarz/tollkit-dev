About paying

The free tools work in every app above, straight away.

Paid tools are paid for by the agent itself, in USDC, from its own wallet — no account, no card on file with us. Chat apps like Claude and ChatGPT don't have a wallet built in, so on their own they'll show you the price rather than pay it. An agent you build yourself, or one given a funded wallet, pays per call automatically.
