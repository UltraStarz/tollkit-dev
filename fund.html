1 · Give your agent a wallet

The easiest way is to let Coinbase do it. Your agent gets a wallet, you add money with a card, Apple Pay or your Coinbase account, and Coinbase keeps the keys safe. Tollkit never touches any of it. Pick where your agent runs:

Chat appsClaude, ChatGPT, Cursor

Coinbase Wallet MCP connects your own Coinbase Wallet. Your AI asks, you tap Approve on each payment.

  1. Add a connector named Coinbase Wallet MCP with this address:
    https://mcp.base.org
    In Claude you can add it in one click.
  2. Press Connect, then Allow in Coinbase Wallet.
  3. Add Tollkit too (how). When a Tollkit tool asks for payment, you get an Approve link.

Coding agentsClaude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI

Coinbase Agentic Wallet gives the agent its own wallet with spending limits. It pays by itself, up to the limits you set.

  1. Install it (needs Node.js 24 or newer):
    npx skills add coinbase/agentic-wallet-skills
  2. Sign in with your email: npx awal auth login you@example.com, then enter the 6-digit code it sends.
  3. Add money: npx awal show opens the wallet. Press Fund.
  4. Paste its address (npx awal address) below to watch its balance and get low-balance alerts.

4 · Fund and refill

Top each agent up to the target. Anything below the floor counts as low. On Base this is two wallet confirmations however many agents you have.

Put in what you'd be annoyed to lose, not what you plan to spend. An agent that reads web pages can be talked into spending by a web page. Its balance is your real limit.

Straight answers

Does Tollkit hold my money?
No. Money moves from your wallet to your agents' wallets in transactions you sign. It never passes through an address we control.
Does Tollkit have my agents' keys?
No. With the Coinbase options, Coinbase keeps the keys. If you make keys yourself on this page, they're generated in your browser and downloaded to your computer. Either way we never see them and couldn't recover them if you asked.
What stops an agent overspending?
Arithmetic first: a wallet can't send more than it holds, so to cut an agent off, stop refilling it. Coinbase adds its own guardrails on top — spending caps for Agentic Wallet, and your Approve tap on every payment for Wallet MCP.
Why do I approve an exact amount?
On Base, funding many agents uses Disperse, a widely used public contract, in one transaction. You approve it for exactly this send — never an unlimited amount — so nothing is left standing afterwards.
What does it cost?
Nothing to us. Base charges well under a cent to send, paid from your wallet's ETH.
