How it works

1

Connect

Press Connect to your AI, pick your app, follow two or three steps.

2

Fund a wallet

Put a few dollars of USDC on Base in a wallet your agent can use. No ETH needed.

Fund a wallet
3

Your agent pays per call

Each call shows its price first. Your agent pays, gets the result. Failed calls cost nothing.

Our promises

  • Failed calls are free. Blocked, dead or unreadable pages cost nothing.
  • No wrong answers passed off as right. If a product link redirects somewhere else, we refuse instead of guessing.
  • Nothing invented. A detail the page doesn’t state comes back empty, not made up.
  • Cached answers say so, with when they were really fetched.
  • Proof anyone can check, free and without trusting us.

For developers: OpenAPI · llms.txt · x402 manifest · signing address · built on x402, settles in USDC on Base.
