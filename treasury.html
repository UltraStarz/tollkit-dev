Treasury

TOLLkit's fee revenue on Base, Ethereum and Solana, and a weekly sweep that turns it into USDC. Everything is signed in your own wallet. This page never sees a key, and TOLLkit's fee wallets pay no TOLLkit fee on their own swaps.

Each week

  1. Claim Jupiter's fees on the referral dashboard (Ultra → Claim). They land in the Solana fee wallet.
  2. Connect the fee wallets below and scan.
  3. Convert everything over the minimum to USDC. Network-fee reserves of ETH and SOL are kept.
  4. Download the report for your books.
