Tollkit Wallet

A self-custody wallet for Base, Ethereum and Solana that stays open in Chrome's side panel, next to the site you're using.

  • Hold ETH, SOL, USDC, USDT and other tokens on Base, Ethereum and Solana from one 12-word recovery phrase.
  • Send, receive, swap and buy. Every swap shows Tollkit's 0.5% fee and the minimum you'll get before you confirm.
  • Cash out to your bank or PayPal through Coinbase, move USDC and ETH between networks, and stake SOL.
  • Spending limits like the Tollkit app: a cap per swap and per day, and your password for bigger swaps and sends.
  • Connect to apps that support MetaMask or Phantom-style wallets. Each connection, signature and transaction waits for your OK in the panel, with the site's name and what it's asking for.
  • Your phrase is encrypted in your browser with your password. Tollkit never has it.
  • Already use the Tollkit app, MetaMask or Phantom? Import your recovery phrase (or the Tollkit app's two backup keys) and you get the same addresses.
