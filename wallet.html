Tollkit Wallet

A self-custody crypto wallet that stays open in Chrome's side panel, next to the site you're on. Your keys stay in your browser, and you set the limits on what can be spent.

One wallet, three networks

Everything in the side panel

ETH, SOL, USDC, USDT and other tokens on Base, Ethereum and Solana, all from one 12-word recovery phrase. Tokens you hold show up on their own.

  • Buy through Coinbase, straight to your own address.
  • Swap, with Tollkit's fee and the minimum you'll receive shown before you confirm.
  • Cash out to your bank or PayPal through Coinbase.
  • Move USDC and ETH between networks, and stake SOL.
  • Connect to apps that work with MetaMask or Phantom. Every request waits for your OK in the panel.

Adding a token by name or address runs Tollkit's token check first, which says what it found in plain words: whether there's a pool to sell it into, or whether its owner can create more.

Bots

Trading bots that show their numbers

Plain rule-based bots, no AI involved: steady buys on a schedule, buying dips, stop-losses and take-profits, profit ladders and grids. You see every setting before a bot starts.

  • Each bot shows its trades, failures and result.
  • The autopilot checks prices every 15 seconds while the wallet is open.
  • See how bots do for other people on the bot board.
Connect your AI

Your AI suggests, you decide

Claude reads your balances and recent prices and suggests rules. Nothing turns on until you tap it. Connected AI apps work inside the same limits, can't withdraw, and never see your keys.

  • Use your own Claude or ChatGPT at no extra cost, or Tollkit's built-in Claude at $0.02 a step, paid from the wallet in USDC.
  • One tap copies your private address and opens Claude's connectors page. Paste it and click Add.
  • The wallet shows Connected once your AI app actually reaches it.
  • Using Tollkit's Claude with no USDC on hand? It offers to swap a little SOL or ETH first, and can do it automatically after you say yes once.

Limits you set

The wallet, its bots and any AI you connect all work inside the same rules. Changing them needs your password.

Caps

A most-per-swap and a most-per-day for trading, and separate caps for paying for tools.

Approvals

Swaps over the amount you pick wait for your OK in the panel or on Telegram.

Slippage

A maximum price movement per swap, so a trade doesn't fill far from its quote.

Password for withdrawals

Sending funds out needs your password every time, so an AI or bot can't move money out of the wallet.

Your keys

Your recovery phrase is encrypted in your browser with your password. Tollkit never has it.

Same addresses

Import a phrase from MetaMask, Phantom or the Tollkit app and you get the same addresses.
