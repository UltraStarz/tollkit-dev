Autopilot

Strategies that change with the market

Every half hour, Autopilot reads a market snapshot: prices, funding, open interest, Fear & Greed, a mood read of the news, and Farcaster. When something moved, it asks the AI for one decision: adjust a strategy, pause it, retire it, or start a new one. Every new strategy trades pretend money first.

  • Goes live only after it beat holding USDC after fees for the paper period you set. A live strategy that's losing goes back to paper.
  • Every decision is logged with its reasons and the signals behind it. Tap one to read them.
  • You set the budget, a cap per trade, a cap per token, and daily and weekly loss stops that pause everything. One switch turns it all off.
  • Decides with your own AI key, only when the market moved. No TOLLkit fee.
  • Thinks while the app is open. Turn on Run 24/7 and its live Base ETH and Solana SOL strategies keep trading while it's closed.
One wallet, three networks

Everything in the side panel

ETH, SOL, USDC, USDT and other tokens on Base, Ethereum and Solana, all from one 12-word recovery phrase. Tokens you hold show up on their own.

  • Buy through Coinbase, straight to your own address.
  • Swap, with TOLLkit's fee and the minimum you'll receive shown before you confirm.
  • Cash out to your bank or PayPal through Coinbase.
  • Move USDC and ETH between networks.
  • Connect to apps that work with MetaMask or Phantom. Every request waits for your OK in the panel.

Adding a token by name or address runs TOLLkit's token check first, which says what it found in plain words: whether there's a pool to sell it into, or whether its owner can create more.

Bots

Trading bots that show their numbers

Plain rule-based bots, no AI involved: buying dips, profit ladders and grids. Simpler one-off and scheduled trades live under Orders: steady buys, stop-losses, take-profits, trailing stops and price or indicator alerts. You see every setting before anything starts.

  • Each bot shows its trades, failures and result, on a price chart that marks where it buys and sells.
  • Before a bot starts, drag its lines on the chart to set it up, and replay it over the last 30 days of prices.
  • Bots check prices every 15 seconds while the wallet is open.
  • See how bots do for other people on the bot board.
Connect your AI

Your AI sets it up. You decide.

Claude reads your balances and recent prices and sets up the swap, rule or bot you describe. It doesn't pick coins or prices for you, and nothing turns on until you tap it. Connected AI apps work inside the same limits, can't withdraw, and never see your keys.

  • Chat in the wallet with your own key from Claude, ChatGPT or OpenRouter, using any model it offers. Your AI company bills you directly.
  • One tap copies your private address and opens Claude's connectors page. Paste it and click Add.
  • The wallet shows Connected once your AI app actually reaches it.

Run 24/7

Orders, bots and live Autopilot strategies on ETH (Base) and SOL (Solana) can keep running on TOLLkit's server while your laptop or phone is off. It's off until you switch it on for each one.

Limits the chain enforces

On Base your wallet signs permission slips for each trade's coin, amount, floor price and time window. On Solana it approves a capped allowance. TOLLkit can't go past either.

Coins come back to you

Every trade swaps inside your own wallet, and the server checks the result goes to your address before it signs. Your keys never leave your device.

Off any time

Switch it off on a rule, or Revoke in Settings, and the permissions are cancelled. TOLLkit pays the network fee for these trades.

Limits you set

The wallet, its bots and any AI you connect all work inside the same rules. Changing them needs your password.

Autopilot budget

A total it may put into live strategies, a cap per trade and per token, and daily and weekly loss stops.

Caps

A most-per-swap and a most-per-day for trading, a slippage cap, and separate caps for paying for tools.

Approvals

Swaps over the amount you pick wait for your OK in the panel or on Telegram.

Password for withdrawals

Sending funds out needs your password every time, so an AI or bot can't move money out of the wallet.

Your keys

Your recovery phrase is encrypted on your device with your password. TOLLkit never has it.

Same addresses

Import a phrase from MetaMask, Phantom or the TOLLkit app and you get the same addresses.

Fees

Every fee is shown before you confirm.

Swaps0.5% of the trade, included in the quote
Trades made by Run 24/7The same 0.5%. TOLLkit pays the network fee.
Moving USDC or ETH between networks0.25%, plus the bridge's own fee
Your own AI (chat and Autopilot)No TOLLkit fee. Your AI company bills you.
Buying and cashing out with CoinbaseCoinbase shows its own fee and the amount before you confirm
Sending and receivingNo TOLLkit fee. The network's own fee applies.

Paper results run a little better than live ones, because pretend trades fill at the quote and real ones can slip. Nothing in the wallet is financial advice. Crypto can lose most of its value quickly; only use money you can afford to lose.
