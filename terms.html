TOLLkit API terms of service

For TOLLkit’s paid tools: the HTTP API on api, web, data, chain and attest.tollkit.dev, and the MCP server at api.tollkit.dev/mcp. Effective October 20, 2026. TOLLkit Wallet has its own terms.

In short: no account needed. Each paid call has a fixed price in USDC, shown in a quote before you pay, and you pay it from your own wallet with the x402 protocol. A call that fails isn’t charged. Payments on the blockchain are final. Answers come from public data, outside services and AI, so they can be wrong or late; check what matters before you rely on it.

3. Prices and payment

  • Fixed price per call. Each tool’s price is listed on the tools page and in every quote. A paid tool called without a payment returns only a quote; nothing is charged.

  • Payments are final. A settled payment is a blockchain transaction and can’t be reversed. If you believe you were charged for a call that didn’t answer, email hello@tollkit.dev with the transaction ID and we’ll look into it.

  • Price changes. We may change prices. A change applies only to calls quoted after it.

Check anything important before relying on it.
