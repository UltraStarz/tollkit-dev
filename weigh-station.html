What it does

Find a paid tool. Describe the job (“product price lookup”, “send SMS”). You get matching services from Coinbase’s public x402 index, each checked live.

Check before paying. For any x402 address: the live price, the network, and the wallet the money goes to. If that wallet differs from the public listing, you get a clear warning.

Neutral. Results are ordered by whether a service answered with a live price, then by how many different wallets have paid it. No paid placement, and Tollkit’s own tools get no boost.

For sellers: monitoring

$5 per endpoint, per 30 days · paid in USDC · nothing renews by itself

We check your endpoint every 15 minutes and send a signed webhook when something changes:

DownAfter two failed checks in a row. Once, not every 15 minutes.
Back upWhen it recovers.
Wrong walletIf your quote starts asking buyers to pay a wallet that isn’t yours.
Price changedWhen the amount you quote changes.
ExpiringThree days before your 30 days end.
Public recordState and 30-day uptime anyone can look up. Never your webhook.

Sign up in three steps

  1. Prepare (free). We read the wallet your endpoint asks buyers to pay. 
    curl -s -X POST https://extract.tollkit.dev/weigh/prepare \
  -H 'content-type: application/json' \
  -d '{"url":"https://your-api.com/endpoint","webhookUrl":"https://your-app.com/hooks/weigh"}'
  2. Sign the returned message with that same wallet. Free, moves no money, and proves the endpoint is yours.
  3. Pay $5: POST the same fields plus signature to /weigh/subscribe with any x402 client. A bad signature or wallet mismatch is refused and never charged.

Look up any endpoint’s record: GET https://extract.tollkit.dev/weigh/status?url=…

Questions

Does checking cost anything or pay the service?
No. We send one unpaid request and read the price it quotes. Nothing is ever paid.
Can a seller pay to rank higher?
No. Monitoring buys alerts and a status record. It never changes what the checks report or where a service ranks.
Does it cover services that aren’t Tollkit’s?
Yes. Any x402 service. Tollkit’s own tools are checked and ranked by the same rules.
What does the wrong-wallet warning mean?
The service’s live price quote asks buyers to pay a different wallet from the one in its public listing. Confirm the recipient before paying.
