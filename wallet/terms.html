For the TOLLkit Wallet browser extension and apps. Effective October 20, 2026.

In short: TOLLkit Wallet is software that runs on your device. You hold your own keys and funds; TOLLkit never does, can’t recover them, and can’t undo a transaction. Every fee is shown before you confirm. Prices move, bots and AI can be wrong, and you’re responsible for what you approve.

2. What TOLLkit Wallet is

  • Self-custody. Your recovery phrase and keys are created and kept on your device, encrypted with your password. TOLLkit never has them and never holds your funds.
  • No recovery. If you lose your recovery phrase and password, TOLLkit can’t restore your wallet. Nobody from TOLLkit will ever ask for your phrase.
  • No undo. Blockchain transactions are final. TOLLkit can’t cancel, reverse or refund a transaction you approved, including one sent to a wrong address.
  • Not a bank, broker or exchange. TOLLkit doesn’t hold deposits, execute trades for you as a broker, or run an exchange. Swaps are filled by outside services on the blockchain, from your own wallet.

4. Fees

Every fee is shown before you confirm.

  • Swaps: 0.5% of the trade, included in the quote. The swap service may charge its own fee, also included in the quote; for example, 0x charges 0.15% on swaps of some token pairs.
  • Moving USDC or ETH between networks: 0.25%, plus the bridge’s own fee.
  • TOLLkit’s built-in AI: $0.02 a step, or $0.04 a step on the Smart model, paid from your wallet in USDC. Using your own AI app costs nothing extra.
  • Buying and cashing out: handled by Coinbase, which shows its own fees and amounts before you confirm.
  • Sending, receiving and staking: no TOLLkit fee. The network’s own fee applies.

We may change our fees. A change applies only to transactions quoted after it.

5. Other services the wallet uses

Some features rely on services TOLLkit doesn’t run, each with its own terms: Coinbase (buying and cashing out, including its identity checks), 0x and Jupiter (swaps; Solana swaps are powered by Jupiter), Circle and Across (moving funds between networks), Anthropic’s Claude (the built-in AI), and the Base, Ethereum and Solana networks. We don’t control them and aren’t responsible for their availability, prices, decisions or errors.

6. Swaps, bots and automation

  • You choose every bot, rule and setting, and the limits it runs inside. Bots follow your rules; they don’t predict prices.
  • Prices can change between a quote and a trade. A swap fills within the slippage you set or doesn’t fill. A bot may fill late, at a worse price, or not at all, for example when the wallet is closed, a network is busy, or you don’t have enough for fees.
  • Charts, replays over past prices, the bot board and other people’s results are information, not a promise of future results.
  • Keep running when closed (optional, off unless you turn it on): you sign permissions on the blockchain that let TOLLkit’s server place only the swaps your rules describe, from your own wallet, within the token, amount, time and minimum-received limits you signed. You can revoke them at any time. TOLLkit’s server can be down or delayed, so a rule may not run on time or at all.

7. AI features

When you use the built-in chat you’re talking to an AI (Claude, made by Anthropic). AI can be wrong or out of date, so check important facts yourself before relying on them. AI suggestions are general information, not personal advice about what to buy or sell. Nothing the AI suggests runs until you approve it, and an AI connected to your wallet works inside your limits and can’t withdraw funds.
